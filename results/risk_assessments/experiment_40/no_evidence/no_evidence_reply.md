## Risk Assessment

**Level:** Medium

**## Analysis**

**Apparent objective:** To inform users about an upcoming migration of the "popsprd" system and to provide instructions for users who use personal spreadsheets with "pops tags."

**Requested action:**  Users are encouraged to call Steve if they have questions about the migration.

**Relevant techniques:**
* **Urgency:** The message states that the migration will occur on April 26th, implying a limited timeframe for users to prepare.
* **Authority:** The sender identifies themselves as "Steve," suggesting an authoritative position within the organization.

**Risk indicators:**
* The message lacks specific details about the migration process and potential impacts beyond the change in node name for "pops tags." 
* The request for users to call Steve could be a pretext to gather more information or credentials.

**Contextual factors:**
* The technical jargon ("popsprd," "nahou - scacll," "pops tags") suggests an internal communication within a specific organization or system.

**Potential consequences:** 
Users who do not update their spreadsheets with the new node name may experience issues accessing or updating their data after the migration.  There is also a potential risk of phishing if users are tricked into providing sensitive information to Steve.


**## Justification**

The message exhibits some characteristics that could be indicative of social engineering, such as urgency and an appeal to authority. However, it lacks overt malicious intent or specific requests for sensitive information. The technical context suggests this is likely an internal communication, but the lack of detail about the migration raises concerns. 


**## Preliminary Guidance**

* Verify Steve's identity through official channels before contacting them.
* Seek further clarification from your organization's IT department regarding the migration process and its potential impact on your work.
* Exercise caution when providing any personal or sensitive information in response to this message.
