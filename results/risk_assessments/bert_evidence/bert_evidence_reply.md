## Risk Assessment

**Level:** Critical

**## Analysis**

**Apparent objective:** To convince the recipient to provide personal and financial information under the pretense of a lucrative opportunity.

**Requested action:** The recipient is asked to supply their fax number and bank routing instructions via a provided URL. 

**Relevant techniques:**
* **Transactional:**  The message frames itself as a business transaction requiring immediate action.
* **Authority:** The sender claims to be "Dr. <PERSON>" and alludes to a legitimate financial institution, attempting to establish authority.
* **Urgency:** Phrases like "strict conditions of confidentiality," "immediate wire remittance," and "secure your 30% allocation" create a sense of urgency and pressure to act quickly.
* **Pretexting:** The entire message is built on a fabricated scenario involving a dormant capital sum and the need for a foreign partner, designed to mislead the recipient.

**Risk indicators:**
*  The sender's email address lacks professional formatting. 
* The request for sensitive information (fax number, bank routing) via an unverified URL is highly suspicious.
* The promise of a large sum of money with minimal effort is a classic red flag for a scam.

**Contextual factors:**
* The message mentions Lagos, Nigeria, which is known as a location associated with various financial scams. 

**Potential consequences:** Providing the requested information could lead to identity theft, financial loss, and potential legal repercussions.

**## Justification**

The message exhibits multiple strong indicators of a social-engineering attack aimed at defrauding the recipient. The combination of urgency, authority claims, pretexting, and requests for sensitive information via an unverified channel strongly suggests malicious intent. 


**## Preliminary Guidance**

* **Do not respond to this message.**
* **Delete the email immediately.**
* **Report the suspicious message to your email provider and relevant authorities.**
